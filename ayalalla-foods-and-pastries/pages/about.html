Our Story

AYLALLA FOODS AND PASTRIES began in a small family kitchen in 1995. What started as a weekend experiment with recipes has grown into a beloved neighborhood bakery, known for our buttery croissants, moist cakes, and artisanal breads. Every generation of the AYLALLA family has contributed secret techniques, passed down like cherished heirlooms.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To bring joy to every table through freshly baked, high‑quality pastries made with love and the finest ingredients.
Vision: To become the most trusted name in artisanal bakery goods, celebrated for tradition, taste, and community.

Family Background

Founded by Maria AYLALLA, a third‑generation baker, the shop is now run together with her children, Lucas and Sofia, who bring modern flair while respecting the old‑world methods. Our team of bakers and chefs share a passion for creating moments of sweetness in people's lives.
